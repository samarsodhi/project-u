### Floor Robotics Scope Boundary & Audit Lock

* **Mandatory Architecture & Selection Gates:**
  1. **De-Marketed Suction Threshold (5,000–7,000 Pa):** 25k Pa marketing claims rejected; airflow and roller agitation govern hard vitrified tile pickup. Owned Dyson V15 covers deep-pile rugs.
  2. **Mandatory Automated Wet Mopping (Active Dual Rotary Mops):** Passive trailing drag pads and dry-only vacuums are strictly disqualified. The machine must feature active counter-rotating spinning mops (180–200 RPM) with automated dock-side pad washing and hot-air drying to clean glazed vitrified tile across 3,435 sq ft.
  3. **Lipid Emulsification (50°C–55°C or Detergent Dosing):** 50°C–55°C warm water or detergent injection required for kitchen tadka/oil residue. 80°C+ scalding claims rejected due to rapid calcification and dock sludge acceleration.
  4. **Washboard Sump Hygiene:** Either a fully detachable 1-piece washboard tray (Roborock architecture) or an automated plumbing hookup kit (direct drain) to prevent anaerobic wastewater sludge.
  5. **Firmware Stability:** Rock-solid LiDAR SLAM navigation; ECOVACS permanently disqualified due to phantom map drift.
  6. **Monsoon Anti-Mildew (Hot-Air Drying $\ge 45^\circ\text{C}$):** Mandatory hot-air pad drying to prevent sour bacterial odors.

* **Explicit Non-Requirements (Locked Against Re-Audit Disqualification):**
  1. **Dock Mop-Detachment Not Required:** Living room 12–15mm wool rug is protected via app boundary ("No-Mop Zone") and spot-cleaned using the owned Dyson V15. Mechanical dock-side mop-dropping mechanisms introduce failure-prone moving parts and are explicitly omitted from selection gates.
  2. **Ultra-Low Profile ($\le 8\text{ cm}$) Not Required:** Standard 9.7–10.3 cm LiDAR turret height clears all Unit B4206 floating consoles (designed at $\ge 200\text{ mm}$ floor clearance). Retractable laser turrets add unneeded mechanical complexity.
