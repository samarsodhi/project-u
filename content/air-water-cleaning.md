### Floor Robotics Scope Boundary & Audit Lock

* **Mandatory Requirements:**
  1. **Hot-Water Mop Washing ($\ge 80^\circ\text{C}$):** Essential for emulsifying Indian cooking oils and turmeric from large vitrified floor tiles. Cold or 50°C pads smear grease across tile grout.
  2. **Active Anti-Tangle Roller (TriCut 3.0 Micro-Blades):** Must slice wrapped hair strands into the airflow to prevent weekly manual hair cutting and axle burnout.
  3. **Hot-Air Pad Drying ($\ge 45^\circ\text{C}$):** Prevents mildew, bacterial growth, and sour odors in humid monsoon conditions.
  4. **Corner & Edge Scrubbing (Extending Swing-Arms):** Physical outward reach for baseboards and kitchen kickboard toe-recesses.
  5. **Hyderabad On-Site Service Network:** First-party warranty and technician presence in Hyderabad (Dreame India network covering 160+ cities; consumables on Amazon Prime).

* **Explicit Non-Requirements (Locked Against Re-Audit Disqualification):**
  1. **Dock Mop-Detachment Not Required:** Living room 12–15mm wool rug is protected via app boundary ("No-Mop Zone") and spot-cleaned using the owned Dyson V15. Mechanical dock-side mop-dropping mechanisms introduce failure-prone moving parts and are explicitly omitted from selection gates.
  2. **Ultra-Low Profile ($\le 8\text{ cm}$) Not Required:** Standard 9.7–10.3 cm LiDAR turret height clears all Unit B4206 floating consoles (designed at $\ge 200\text{ mm}$ floor clearance). Retractable laser turrets add unneeded mechanical complexity.
