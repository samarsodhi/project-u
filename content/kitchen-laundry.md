## 3. Engineering & Site Protocols

### Undermount Sink Cradle (Failure Prevention)
The #1 cause of kitchen cabinet rot is adhesive-only undermount failure.
- **Mechanical Cradle:** Fabricate a welded SS angle-iron support frame bolted directly to the plywood cabinet sidewalls before lowering the granite slab. Never rely solely on adhesive silicone.
- **Akemi Fillet:** Apply a continuous Akemi two-part epoxy fillet between the underside of the stone and the sink rim. Seal the wet-wipe edge with mildew-resistant sanitary silicone.

### Worktop Sealing & Maintenance
- **Granite Treatment:** Light Indian granite must be dry-treated with 2 saturating coats of **Akemi Anti-Fleck Nano Sealer** prior to hob/sink installation. Re-seal every 12–18 months.
- **Hob Cutout:** Cutout for Häfele domino pair (555–557 × 475–477 mm) must have radiused (curved) internal corners. Square 90° cuts initiate stress fractures under thermal cycles.

### Countertop Underside Drip Groove (Water Break)
- **Physics & Failure Mode:** Spilled water clings to polished stone undersides via surface tension, wicking back to cabinetry. In handle-less kitchens, water pools in G-profile channels, rotting shutter edge banding, swelling BWP ply joints, and rusting runners.
- **Overhang Dimension:** Stone slab must project 35–50 mm (1.5–2 inches) beyond finished cabinet shutter faces. Overhangs under 30 mm drop water directly onto cabinet doors.
- **Groove Specification:** Cut a 3–5 mm wide by 3–5 mm deep groove 10–15 mm inward from the slab underside front edge. The 90° undercut breaks surface tension, forcing runoff directly to the floor.
- **Stone Thickness Safety:** On single-layer 18–20 mm granite, limit groove depth to 3–4 mm to prevent chipping the front lip. Double-molded edges safely accept full 5 mm depth.

### Drinking Water RO Engineering & Hygiene Protocol
Microbial hygiene is the primary non-negotiable selection criterion for drinking water.
- **Biofilm Disqualification:** Hydro-pneumatic under-sink pressure tanks store unchlorinated pure RO permeate against dark butyl rubber at 25–35°C. This develops bacterial biofilm within 6–12 months that cannot be mechanically cleaned. Disqualify all hydro-pneumatic rubber bladder tanks.
- **Sealed SS304 Storage vs. Detachable Mechanisms:** Wall-mounted sealed surgical AISI 304 tanks with automated in-tank UV-C sterilization (LG PuriCare WW184ETB) eliminate bacterial biofilm without introducing physical handling hazards. Detachable tanks (BlueLife Gigas) require lifting 11–13 kg drums at chest height and suffer silicone docking gasket abrasion and bypass leaks.
- **TDS Creep Protocol (Direct Tankless):** High-flux membranes (600–800 GPD) equilibrate salt ions across the barrier when idle. The first 250–500 ml dispensed after 4+ hours idle spikes to 200–350 ppm TDS. If installing tankless under-sink, run the tap for 15–20 seconds before filling a drinking glass.
- **Anti-DRM Filter Mandate:** Ban all purifiers with RFID microchip cartridges (e.g. Aquaguard) or hard software lockouts that brick water flow. Require manual timer resets (e.g. LG 3-second Child Lock hold). Protect inlet with an external 10-inch sediment pre-filter and point-of-use PRV.
- **Point-of-Use Pressure Protection:** High-rise hydro-pneumatic water lines experience pressure surges (>4 bar) that blow plastic push-fit tubing. Mount an external transparent 10" sediment pre-filter and a dedicated point-of-use PRV (Zoloto Art. 1040B forged brass or inline 1/4" PRV calibrated to 2.5–3.0 bar) directly ahead of the purifier inlet.
- **LG Maintenance & AMC Optimization Protip:** Decline the ₹5,000/year AMC contract after Year 1. Mount an external 10-inch transparent spun polypropylene pre-filter bowl ahead of the purifier inlet. Swap the external ₹100 spun candle quarterly to catch 95% of suspended silt, extending internal LG filter life to 18–24 months. Exploit zero RFID DRM on LG by resetting the countdown timer with a 3-second button hold. Order genuine LG replacement filters on-demand (or book a single service visit) strictly when tested TDS or flow rate warrants, cutting upkeep from ₹5,500 to ~₹1,800/year.

### Laundry Stack Site Rules
- Stack heat-pump dryer over front-load washer using the official Bosch stacking kit (WTZ27400).
- Mount directly on structural tile floor using heavy rubber anti-vibration isolation pads. Never place on elevated hollow wooden plinths.

### Utility Parapet Enclosure (Floor 42 Engineering & FM Rider)
- **FM Façade Mandate & Community Precedent:** Sumadhura FM permits enclosing the utility aperture strictly using **White uPVC** matching the tower exterior elevation (disallowing non-matching aluminium; see olympus-community-qa). Verified vendor lead: UPVC Works (Kukatpally via S V V Satyanarayana in olympus-vendor-directory).
- **High-Rise Structural Spec:** Enforce **$\ge 1.5\text{ mm}$ hot-dip galvanized steel core reinforcement** inside all uPVC chambers and **$\ge 6\text{ mm}$ clear toughened glass** (IS 875 wind-load compliance). Anchor frame into RCC lintel and concrete parapet with Fischer sleeve anchors ($\le 450\text{ mm}$ pitch). Bottom sill track must feature factory weep holes with external gravity flaps.
- **3-Track Ventilation & Make-Up Air (MUA) Rule:** Mandate a **3-track system (2 glass sliders + 1 SS304 mesh)**. Never install fixed, non-openable glass. Slide one sash open $\ge 50\text{ mm}$ onto the SS304 mesh screen during Bosch heat-pump dryer cycles (keeping utility temps $<38^\circ\text{C}$), during Speed 2/3 kitchen chimney extraction (supplying Make-Up Air so negative pressure does not suck sewer odor through bathroom traps), and to comply with PNGRB gas meter ventilation rules.
