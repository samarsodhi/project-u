# Kitchen & Laundry

Technical specification for cabinetry, worktop, SS 304 sink, hybrid hobs, extraction, drinking water, and laundry utility.

> **Homz Joinery Scope:** No builder granite (owner Ivory Fantasy); no built-in dishwasher (convert bay to tandem drawers); freestanding step bin (no under-sink bin pull-out).

Top reasons to reject or alter this specification: (1) omitting $\ge 5\text{ mm}$ hob cutout radii cracks stone and hanging undermount sinks without a welded SS304 cradle causes detachment; (2) butyl rubber RO bladders breed biofilm and under-sink bins trap rot; (3) skipping bi-weekly chimney thermal auto-clean or running heat-pump dryers in $>35^\circ\text{C}$ unventilated rooms causes premature failure.

## 2. Engineering & Site Protocols

* **Undermount Sink Cradle & Stone Geometry:** Bolt a welded AISI 304 angle-iron cradle to cabinet sidewalls before placing stone and seal the rim with Akemi two-part epoxy + sanitary silicone. Pre-treat Ivory Fantasy granite with 2 coats of Akemi Nano Sealer. Project slabs 35–50 mm past shutters with a 3–5×3–4 mm underside drip groove 10–15 mm inward. Cut two $270 \times 490\text{ mm}$ hob openings with a 50–70 mm stone bridge and $\ge 5\text{ mm}$ diamond-cored corner radii.
* **RO Hygiene & Cabinet Clearances:** Connect `LG WW184ETB` behind an external 10" 5-micron pre-filter and 2.5–3.0 bar PRV; route reject water via an air-gap clamp $\ge 50\text{ mm}$ above the P-trap (decline ₹5k AMC; swap ₹100 candle quarterly and reset via 3s hold per [[finalized-purchases]]). Fit a 12 mm FR ply vertical baffle between gas and induction bays, a 12 mm heat shield 60 mm below the induction hob (5 mm front intake gap), 50 mm rear PNG grommets, and cabinet skirting bridged over the 75 mm concrete drain curb without chipping.
* **Utility Parapet Enclosure (Floor 42):** Sumadhura FM mandates **White uPVC only** (olympus-community-qa; vendor: UPVC Works Kukatpally in olympus-vendor-directory). Specify $\ge 1.5\text{ mm}$ GI core, $\ge 6\text{ mm}$ toughened glass, Fischer sleeve anchors ($\le 450\text{ mm}$ pitch), weep flaps, and a 3-track slider (2 glass + 1 SS304 mesh) opened $\ge 50\text{ mm}$ during dryer/chimney cycles.
