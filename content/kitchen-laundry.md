# Kitchen & Laundry

Technical specification for cabinetry, worktop, SS 304 sink, hybrid hobs, extraction, drinking water, and laundry utility.

> **Locked Scope Decisions:** Builder granite removed. Homz frames carcasses for owner-sourced light stone (Ivory Fantasy granite). Sink is strictly 16-gauge (1.2 mm) AISI 304 stainless steel undermount; composite sinks are disqualified. No built-in dishwasher; bay converted to tandem cutlery and utensil drawers. Step waste bin remains freestanding outside cabinetry.

Top reasons to reject or alter this specification:
1. Structural failure: Omitting $\ge 5\text{ mm}$ stress-relief radii on hob cutouts cracks stone. Relying on silicone without a welded SS angle cradle collapses undermount sinks.
2. Microbial contamination: Pressurized butyl rubber RO tanks breed unscrubbable biofilm. Enclosed under-sink bins trap moisture, rot, and pests.
3. Appliance breakdown: Auto-clean chimneys bake tadka oil into varnish. Operating heat-pump dryers above 35°C in unventilated rooms doubles cycle times and stresses compressors.

## 2. Engineering & Site Protocols

### Undermount Sink Cradle
- **Mechanical Frame:** Fabricate a welded AISI 304 angle-iron frame bolted directly to cabinet sidewalls before placing stone. Never hang an undermount sink on adhesive silicone alone.
- **Epoxy Fillet:** Run a continuous Akemi two-part epoxy fillet between stone underside and sink rim. Seal the wet-wipe seam with anti-fungal sanitary silicone.

### Worktop Sealing & Cutout Geometry
- **Granite Protection:** Dry-treat light granite with 2 saturating coats of Akemi Anti-Fleck Nano Sealer before cutouts; re-seal every 12–18 months.
- **Hob Cutouts:** Cut two independent $270 \times 490\text{ mm}$ openings separated by a continuous $50\text{–}70\text{ mm}$ granite bridge. Diamond-core internal corners to radius $\ge 5\text{ mm}$; sharp 90° corners fracture under thermal expansion.

### Countertop Underside Drip Groove (Water Break)
- **Overhang:** Project stone slabs 35–50 mm beyond finished cabinet shutter faces to shield G-profile channels.
- **Groove Specification:** Grind a continuous 3–5 mm wide × 3–4 mm deep groove 10–15 mm inward from the slab underside front edge. The 90° undercut breaks surface tension and stops water wicking back into carcass ply joints. On single-layer 20 mm granite, cap groove depth at 4 mm to protect the front lip.

### Drinking Water & RO Hygiene
- **Storage Hygiene:** Disqualify pressurized butyl-rubber bladder tanks; dark unchlorinated water breeds bacterial biofilm within 12 months.
- **Tank Architecture:** LG PuriCare WW184ETB provides an airtight 8L surgical SS 304 tank with automated 6-hour UV-C LED sterilization. Plugs into builder splashback bibcock and 6A socket without countertop core drilling.
- **Pressure Protection:** High-rise water surges (>4 bar) burst push-fit tubing. Mount an external 10\" sediment pre-filter and point-of-use PRV calibrated to 2.5–3.0 bar directly ahead of the purifier inlet.
- **Maintenance & AMC Protocol:** Decline the ₹5,000/yr AMC contract. Swap the external ₹100 spun candle quarterly to catch 95% of silt, extending internal filter life to 24 months. Reset the LG filter timer via a 3-second button hold. Buy genuine filter cartridges on-demand when TDS warrants, cutting annual upkeep to ~₹1,800.
- **Direct Tankless TDS Creep:** If installing tankless RO systems, run the tap for 15–20 seconds before filling a glass; membranes equilibrate salt ions while idle, spiking initial output to 200–350 ppm TDS.

### Utility Parapet Enclosure (Floor 42)
- **Façade Mandate:** Sumadhura FM permits White uPVC only (olympus-community-qa). Non-matching aluminium is prohibited. Verified vendor lead: UPVC Works (Kukatpally via S V V Satyanarayana in olympus-vendor-directory).
- **Structural Spec:** Enforce $\ge 1.5\text{ mm}$ hot-dip galvanized steel core reinforcement inside uPVC profiles and $\ge 6\text{ mm}$ clear toughened glass (IS 875 wind-load compliant). Anchor frame to RCC with Fischer sleeve anchors ($\le 450\text{ mm}$ pitch). Sill tracks require weep holes with external gravity flaps.
- **3-Track Ventilation:** Mandate 2 glass sliding panels + 1 SS 304 mesh screen. Keep one sash open $\ge 50\text{ mm}$ onto the mesh during dryer cycles (caps utility temperature below 35°C) and chimney extraction (Make-Up Air prevents sewer trap backdrafts).

### Cabinet Thermal & Service Clearances
- **Hob Thermal Separation:** Install a 12 mm fire-retardant ply vertical baffle between the gas and induction bays.
- **Induction Heat Shield:** Fix a 12 mm horizontal intermediate shelf 60 mm below the induction unit with a 5 mm front intake air gap. Isolates cooling fans from top-drawer cutlery and foil.
- **Service Clearances:** Cut 50 mm circular grommets in rear casework for PNG pipe runs. Frame base cabinet skirting over the 75 mm raised floor drain curb without chipping concrete.
