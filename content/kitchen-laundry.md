## 3. Engineering & Site Protocols

### Undermount Sink Cradle (Failure Prevention)
The #1 cause of kitchen cabinet rot is adhesive-only undermount failure.
- **Mechanical Cradle:** Fabricate a welded SS angle-iron support frame bolted directly to the plywood cabinet sidewalls before lowering the granite slab. Never rely solely on adhesive silicone.
- **Akemi Fillet:** Apply a continuous Akemi two-part epoxy fillet between the underside of the stone and the sink rim. Seal the wet-wipe edge with mildew-resistant sanitary silicone.

### Worktop Sealing & Maintenance
- **Granite Treatment:** Light Indian granite must be dry-treated with 2 saturating coats of **Akemi Anti-Fleck Nano Sealer** prior to hob/sink installation. Re-seal every 12–18 months.
- **Hob Cutout:** Cutout for Häfele domino pair (555–557 × 475–477 mm) must have radiused (curved) internal corners. Square 90° cuts initiate stress fractures under thermal cycles.

### Laundry Stack Site Rules
- Stack heat-pump dryer over front-load washer using the official Bosch stacking kit (WTZ27400).
- Mount directly on structural tile floor using heavy rubber anti-vibration isolation pads. Never place on elevated hollow wooden plinths.

### Worktop & Domino Hob Fit-Out
- [ ] **Slab Nano-Sealing:** Treat owner-sourced light granite with 2 saturating coats of **Akemi Anti-Fleck Nano Sealer** before cutting appliance openings.
- [ ] **Hob Cutout Strategy (Homz / Stone Mason):**
  - **Preferred (Granite Bridge):** Cut two independent rectangular openings ($265 \times 475\text{ mm}$ for Altius FS 230; $260 \times 490\text{ mm}$ for Bella 30) separated by a 50–70 mm continuous granite bridge.
  - **Alternative (Joining Rail):** If using single cutout with Häfele joining strip, execute a stepped cut ($475\text{ mm}$ depth left, $490\text{ mm}$ depth right) and seal seams with Dow Corning 736 high-temp silicone.
  - **Stress Relief:** Ensure all internal cutout corners are diamond-cored with $\ge 5\text{ mm}$ radius.
- [ ] **Cabinet Thermal Separation:** Install a 12 mm fire-retardant ply baffle between the gas bay and induction bay. Maintain $\ge 50\text{ mm}$ drop clearance and 5 mm front intake air gap for Bella 30 fan.

### Under-Sink RO & Wet Services
- [ ] **MEP Relocation into Sink Base:** Relocate builder's high-level RO water inlet and 6A power socket from the splashback down inside the sink base cabinet.
- [ ] **RO Faucet Deck Hole:** Factory wet-core a 12 mm tap hole in the granite before delivery, or install a 3-way mixer tap (hot/cold/RO) to avoid extra stone penetrations.
- [ ] **Undermount Sink Mechanical Frame:** Bolt welded SS angle-iron support cradle to cabinet sidewalls before lowering granite; seal rim with Akemi two-part epoxy fillet.
- [ ] **Air-Gap Drain Saddle:** Mount RO reject line to drain tailpiece via dedicated air gap $\ge 50\text{ mm}$ above P-trap weir.
- [ ] **Flood Containment:** Line sink base carcass floor with an ABS/aluminum drip tray; place an **Aqara Water Leak Sensor** on the tray linked to smart shutoff.
- [ ] **Pressure Regulation:** Confirm flat main PRV is regulated to 2.5–3.0 bar before connecting RO John Guest fittings.
