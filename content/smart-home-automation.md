# Smart Home & Automation

Master technical specification for the local-first automation layer: in-wall Zigbee 3.0 micro-relays behind builder switch plates bridged via Aqara Hub M3 to Matter/Alexa, phase-cut dimmers, video doorbell, and BLDC ceiling fans.

> **Local-First Architecture:** Physical wall switches operate mechanically even if Wi-Fi or internet goes down. In-wall relays standardize 100% on **Zigbee 3.0** (`Aqara T1/T2` with and without neutral) bound directly to the **Aqara Hub M3** (bridged locally to Alexa via Matter over Ethernet). This creates a single, self-healing mesh and eliminates Zigbee bisection and 12–18 dBm 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi attenuation inside grounded 50 mm GI metal backboxes.

## BLDC Ceiling Fan Specifications & Siting Rules

* **Zero Analog Regulators:** Conventional rheostat wall regulators must be completely bypassed in the switchboard. BLDC electronics require constant 230V; feeding chopped voltage destroys the internal inverter PCB.
* **Structural Anchoring & Vibration Isolation:** Never hang fans from false ceiling gypsum or GI framing. Anchor downrods directly to the RCC slab J-hook via heavy-duty MS extensions with dense rubber isolation grommets.
* **False Ceiling Clearances & Anti-Strobe Rules:**
  * **Height:** Maintain 2.45–2.60 m finished floor-to-blade clearance. Size downrods specifically to finished false ceiling drop.
  * **Top Clearance:** Maintain $\ge 250\text{ mm}$ clearance between blades and false ceiling face.
  * **Anti-Strobe Lighting Gate:** Maintain $\ge 600\text{ mm}$ horizontal distance between fan blade tips and any recessed LED downlight cone.
* **Kitchen Interlock Rule:** Never run the kitchen ceiling fan during active frying or tadka; turbulent cross-drafts disrupt chimney suction and disperse aerosolized grease onto overhead cabinets.

## Electrical Pre-Wire & Site Requirements (Homz Scope)
* **MEP & Surge Protection Standards:** Utilize existing 50 mm deep builder GI backboxes (strictly enforce perimeter wire dressing; no box replacement or wall-chasing), room-isolated neutral wires, Type A/F 30mA RCCBs, and primary DB Type 2 40kA SPD per [[gotchas-and-tips#2-electrical-grid-surges--inverter-pcb-protection]].
* **Modular Switchgear Spec & Two-Tone Finish:** Builder-installed plates are **Panasonic** in a two-tone finish (brushed metallic silver frame + matte black/graphite modules). Source exact matching blanking plates and auxiliary switches from **Laxmi Electricals** (300 m from North/East Gate).
* **Dummy Conduit Route Preservation:** Never allow painters to skim plaster flush over builder dummy electrical/data conduits and junction boxes. Fit matching Panasonic two-tone blanking plates and log photo measurements before wall closing so future Cat6 or smart home wiring can be pulled without destructive wall chasing.
