# Smart Home & Automation

Master technical specification for the local-first automation layer: in-wall Matter/Shelly micro-relays behind builder switch plates, Alexa voice controllers, Aqara safety hub, video doorbell, and BLDC ceiling fans.

> **Local-First Architecture:** Physical wall switches operate mechanically even if Wi-Fi or internet goes down. Critical circuits use Shelly Gen3 (persistent state across power outages). Zero wall-chasing; all micro-relays fit inside deep 43 mm GI backboxes.

## BLDC Ceiling Fan Specifications & Siting Rules

* **Zero Analog Regulators:** Conventional rheostat wall regulators must be completely bypassed in the switchboard. BLDC electronics require constant 230V; feeding chopped voltage destroys the internal inverter PCB.
* **Structural Anchoring & Vibration Isolation:** Never hang fans from false ceiling gypsum or GI framing. Anchor downrods directly to the RCC slab J-hook via heavy-duty MS extensions with dense rubber isolation grommets.
* **False Ceiling Clearances & Anti-Strobe Rules:**
  * **Height:** Maintain 2.45–2.60 m finished floor-to-blade clearance. Size downrods specifically to finished false ceiling drop.
  * **Top Clearance:** Maintain $\ge 250\text{ mm}$ clearance between blades and false ceiling face.
  * **Anti-Strobe Lighting Gate:** Maintain $\ge 600\text{ mm}$ horizontal distance between fan blade tips and any recessed LED downlight cone.
* **Kitchen Interlock Rule:** Never run the kitchen ceiling fan during active frying or tadka; turbulent cross-drafts disrupt chimney suction and disperse aerosolized grease onto overhead cabinets.

## Electrical Pre-Wire & Site Requirements (Homz Scope)
* **Backbox Depth:** Mandate **43 mm deep GI concealed switch boxes** across all rooms.
* **Neutral Wire & RCCB Loop Isolation:** Pull a certified neutral wire to every switch gang from that exact room's MCB circuit.
* **Surge Protection:** Equip primary distribution board with Type 2 40kA SPD (Surge Protection Device) to safeguard sensitive micro-relays from grid transients.
