# Turnkey Execution, Contractual Controls & Material Sourcing

Homz Interio executes turnkey fixed woodwork (~₹18L inclusive of GST). Protect project scope against contractor substitution, cost-creep, and Bangalore-jurisdiction clauses through an enforceable contractual rider. **Every material and procurement decision follows High-Quality VFM first, followed by High-Quality Unconstrained.**

## Contract Rider: Twelve Non-Negotiable Clauses
Paste these binding clauses as an annexure into the Homz agreement prior to signing:
1. **Intellectual Property:** Owner retains unrestricted royalty-free rights to all shop drawings and 3D CAD files for future alterations.
2. **Warranty Standards:** 12-month defect liability period on all civil/carpentry workmanship; manufacturer warranty (min 10–30 years) on core boards and hardware.
3. **Variation Ceiling:** Cumulative variations capped at $\le 3\%$ of total contract value; any upward deviation requires prior written owner approval.
4. **Retention Money:** 10% payment milestone retained until complete snag list clearance and final site handover.
5. **Liquidated Damages:** 0.5% contract value penalty per week of unexcused project delay, capped at 10%.
6. **Payment Milestones:** 10% Advance $\rightarrow$ 30% Carcass Framework delivery $\rightarrow$ 30% Shutter Boarding & Plastering $\rightarrow$ 20% Painting & Hardware installation $\rightarrow$ 10% Final Snag Handover.
7. **Legal Jurisdiction:** Exclusive jurisdiction under Courts of **Hyderabad** or binding arbitration under the Arbitration & Conciliation Act 1996 (reject Bangalore default).
8. **Precedence Clause:** This rider supersedes all standard boilerplate terms and conditions.
9. **Soft-Close Runner Inclusion:** All kitchen tandem drawers (2 below hob, 6 flanking sink, 1 replacing wicker basket) and wardrobe drawers must include Hettich/Blum soft-close channels within the ₹18L contract sum (overriding boilerplate normal-close default).
10. **Fixed Calendar Completion:** "60/75/90 working days" boilerplate is capped at **75 calendar days** from site measurement sign-off, ensuring handover by 15 March 2027.
11. **Scope Adjustments Locked:** Base quote deducts the removed L-shaped kitchen granite and dining console, and includes the store room overhead loft, bathroom SS304 curtain rods, and dome-top copper-free mirrors without post-measurement price escalation.
12. **Refund & Cancellation Fairness:** Overrides "all payments non-refundable" clause; if Homz delays design or production >30 days past milestone dates, unexpended stage payments are 100% refundable.

## Site Quality Protocols (Floor 42 Execution)
* **Tile Protection:** Lay 3 mm corrugated polypropylene floor-protection sheets taped with cloth tape over vitrified tiles prior to material unloading.
* **Dust Mitigation:** Wet-cut tiles and stone on the external utility balcony only; seal VRF ceiling return grilles with plastic film during sanding.
* **Service Lift Scheduling:** Book freight lift 3 days in advance with Olympus RWA management; confirm dimensions for items $>2\text{ m}$.
