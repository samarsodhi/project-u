### Engineering & Materials Decision Guide

#### 1. Countertop Comparison
* **Indian Granite (Steel Grey / Absolute Black):**
  * *Heat limit:* >300 °C (direct hot tawa safe).
  * *Porosity & Sealing:* Low (seal every 12–18 months).
  * *Reddit Insight:* The undisputed workhorse for heavy Indian cooking. Test slab with water: if it darkens in <5 min, reject or demand heavy yard sealing.
* **Manufactured Quartz (Pokarna Quantra / Caesarstone):**
  * *Heat limit:* ~150 °C (7% polyester resin burns under hot oil vessels; trivets mandatory).
  * *Porosity & Sealing:* Zero porosity; never needs sealing. Seams are nearly invisible.
* **Sintered Stone (Dekton / Neolith):**
  * *Heat limit:* >500 °C, zero porosity.
  * *Vulnerability:* Extremely brittle during fabrication and installation; corners chip easily if heavy pressure cookers bang against undermount rims.

#### 2. Undermount Sink Installation Protocol
The #1 cause of counter failure is undermount seam leakage rotting BWP plywood cabinetry.
* **Mechanical Framing:** Install a dedicated stainless steel cradle or angle-iron support bolted to the cabinet carcass before setting stone. Never rely solely on adhesive.
* **Epoxy Sealing:** Use Akemi two-part epoxy fillet between sink lip and underside of stone; seal wet-wipe edge with sanitary silicone.
* **Material Spec:** Sinks must be certified AISI 304 grade, minimum 18-gauge (1.0 mm). Reject Grade 202 (rusts within 2 years in municipal water).

#### 3. Trade Touchpoints & Cutouts
* **Masonry & Stone Cutouts:** Confirm hob cutout matches Häfele domino specs (555–557 × 475–477 mm) with rounded internal radius corners to prevent stress crack propagation.
* **Plumbing Clearance:** Route pull-down faucet counterweight hoses clear of AO Smith under-sink RO filters; sink cabinet remains free of trash bins.

**Skip List:** Marble countertops (calcium carbonate dissolves on lemon/vinegar contact), Nano White (brittle crystallization cracks at hob cutouts), Grade 202 stainless steel, non-mechanical silicone-only undermount sink installs.
