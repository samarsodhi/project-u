## 1. Rules for Agents

1. **Scope Control:** Apply owner statements strictly to their explicit context. Do not infer wider policies.
2. **Label Defaults:** Place any assumption not directly decreed by the owner into [§5 Agent Defaults](#5-agent-defaults-to-confirm).
3. **Measurement Dependencies:** Do not research questions that a physical tape measure settles. Mark the gate and pause.
4. **Dual-Tier Matrix:** Every recommendation table must present two tiers: (1) High-Quality VFM First, then (2) High-Quality Unconstrained. Use the standard BIFL+ formula (`0.3·Dur + 0.3·Func + 0.2·VFM + 0.2·Style`).
5. **Sourcing Parity:** Do not default to global retail solely for fetchable URLs. Local makers without online spec sheets get site visits or phone calls.
6. **Instruction & Spec Guardrail (Reddit & Top-Tier SME Standard):** Any entry to this instruction brief or downstream setup notes must satisfy the **Reddit & Top-Tier SME Standard** (cross-verified against real-world Reddit teardowns and passing the bar of what the top field specialist or master architect in India would mandate on site).
7. **One-Row Domain Matrix Template:** All domain setup notes (`u-setup-*.md`) must adhere strictly to the established 4-column matrix: `| Need | Tier 1: High-Quality VFM | Tier 2: High-Quality Unconstrained | Top Failure Mode & Reddit Insight |`. Add exactly **one row per need** containing both tiers and failure modes inline. Never insert standalone multi-row evaluation tables into domain notes.

* **Contract Rider:** Mandatory 12-point rider (milestone payments, GSTIN verification, price caps, snag holdbacks) required before deposit.
* **Timeline:** Target move-in by mid-April 2027. Maintain lease flexibility through August 2027.

### 3.2 Civil & Trades Boundary
* **Builder Finishes:** Retain builder VRF AC, basic wiring, plumbing, and finished bathrooms. Full interior repaint authorized per [[u-setup-interiors#72-interior-wall-paint--color-palette-designer--reddit-standard]].
* **Electrical & Fans:** Fit smart BLDC ceiling fans in all rooms and common spaces per [[u-setup-smart-home#bldc-ceiling-fan-specifications--siting-rules-designer--reddit-standard]]. **Zero wall chasing and no major rewiring.** Add dedicated 16A circuits strictly inside joinery or via false ceilings.

### 3.3 Sourcing & Maker Hierarchy
* **Budget Envelope:** 2× baseline (~₹41–64 Lakh) outside Homz to secure BIFL durability over disposable modular retail.
* **First-Principles Sourcing Guidance (How to Think):**
  * **Material Authenticity Over Label:** Prioritize verified seasoned solid timber (teak, ash, oak) and joinery integrity over brand prestige.
  * **Dual-Tier Sequencing:** Present High-Quality VFM first, followed by High-Quality Unconstrained.
  * **Inversion Before Addition:** Quantify failure modes (child wear, warping, lift jamming) before assessing aesthetics.
  * **Tie-Breaker Rule:** When options score within 0.1 on the BIFL matrix, the higher-quality build wins.
* **Tier 1: High-Quality VFM Archetypes & Examples:**
  * *Direct Timber Workshops:* Procure seasoned Central Province (CP) teak directly. Build heavy casework (beds, prayer units, dining frames) to CAD drawings with local master joiners to bypass 40–50% retail markups.
  * *Tested Modular Workhorses:* Industrial makers with strict European chemical and mechanical safety standards for high-wear zones.
    * *Example:* **IKEA** for 100% washable slipcover sofas (KIVIK), manual-crank child desks (BERGLÄRKA), and modular utility.
  * *Direct-to-Consumer / Export Finishers:* Export manufacturers offering clean Japandi lines, precision metal-wood hybrids, or mill-direct textiles.
    * *Examples:* **[Spin](https://madewithspin.com)** (minimalist powder-coated steel and solid ash), **[Orange Tree](https://www.orangetree.in)** (FSC-certified wood and brass accents), **[Obeetee](https://www.obeetee.in)** / **[Jaipur Rugs](https://www.jaipurrugs.com/in)** (dense low-pile hand-tufted wool).
* **Tier 2: High-Quality Unconstrained Archetypes & Examples:**
  * *Heirloom Design Collectives:* Heritage studios reviving South Asian Modernism and mid-century classics with certified seasoned timber.
    * *Example:* **[Phantom Hands](https://phantomhands.in)** for licensed Pierre Jeanneret and Geoffrey Bawa teak and cane re-editions.
  * *Architectural Woodworking Studios:* In-house timber manufacturing with custom finishes, precision mortise-and-tenon joinery, and showroom sit-tests.
    * *Examples:* **[Alankaram](https://www.alankaram.in)** (solid teak/ash dining and credenzas), **[Magari](https://www.magari.in)** (Jubilee Hills studio for ash lounges), **Quadro Living** (Hyderabad bespoke studio).
  * *Artisanal Lighting & Textile Ateliers:* Custom architectural brass fixtures and high-durability fabrics.
    * *Examples:* **[The Purple Turtles](https://thepurpleturtles.com)** (architectural brass pendants), **Bhavani Furnishings** (Blue Wool 6+ drapery), **[Gulmohar Lane](https://www.gulmoharlane.com)** (loose slipcovered armchairs).
* **Anti-Patterns & De-Marketing Redlines (Looking Around Corners):**
  * *Reject Jodhpur Online Dropshippers:* Unverified online Sheesham/mango wood sellers supply unseasoned sapwood prone to warping and borer pests.
  * *Reject Decor Brands for Structural Wood:* Relegate lifestyle brands (e.g. **Freedom Tree**) to textiles, prints, and ceramics; avoid for heavy structural timber.
  * *Cane / Rattan Rule (Child Kabir Age 6):* Natural cane permitted only on vertical chair backs. Ban cane on horizontal seat pans; children kneeling punch through rattan mesh.
  * *Logistics & Lift Gate:* Ban unibody furniture frames >2.1 m without verified service lift diagonal clearance. Mandate knock-down (KD) bolted assemblies.
  * *Maintenance Tax:* Require machine-washable removable slipcovers for family seating. Apply hydrophobic protection to fixed upholstery on delivery.
  * *Lead-Time Protection:* Enforce written delivery penalty terms on boutique studios (Alankaram, Magari) to prevent 12+ week slippage past April 2027.

### 3.4 Smart Home, AV & Networking
* **Architecture:** Alexa-centric interface with Matter protocol local control.
* **Lighting:** Sonoff MINIR4M Matter relays inside existing switch boxes. Sonoff MINI-DIM for dimmable lamps (zero smart bulbs behind hardware dimmers).
* **Audio:** Whole-home multi-room audio over home theater. Sonos Arc Ultra in Living (paired to owned 65" Sony TV); owned Sonos Era 300 in Drawing.
* **Networking:** Structured Cat6 cabling to 3 ceiling drops (deploy 2 APs at move-in). Gateway: owned GL.iNet Flint 2.

### 3.5 Appliances & Bathrooms
* **Kitchen:** L-shaped granite removed from Homz scope; procure **light-shade Indian granite** directly and seal with fluorochemical nano-sealer. Layout locked: 2 tandem drawers below hob, 3 drawers on either side of sink, and tandem drawer replacing wicker basket (ventilate rear panel for dry produce). Finishes: `3174 LU Brown Sugar` base, `158 LU Taupe` overheads/lofts. **No dishwasher.**
* **Laundry:** Stacked front-load washer + heat-pump dryer on structural floor in covered utility alcove (no hollow plinths).
* **Water Heating:** 10L 2kW Wi-Fi storage geysers (Racold Omnis DG) across all 4 bathrooms (mild Hyderabad winters, 4 washrooms for 3 residents). Exclude 15L and 25L tanks.
* **Showers & Washrooms:** Glass partitions removed to preserve bathroom spaciousness. Install wall-flanged **SS304 curtain rods** with weighted curtains and epoxy-glued floor water threshold bars. Washroom cabinet laminate colors TBD.

### 3.6 Safety & Ergonomics

* **Sensors:** Interlinked Aqara smoke alarms in bedrooms/living; kitchen methane gas detector 30 cm below ceiling.
* **Kabir's Study:** IKEA BERGLÄRKA manual crank desk (52–75 cm) to maintain safe 53 cm seated height.

### 3.7 Drawing Room Wall Treatment (13'0" × 17'8")

* **West Wall (Feature Media):** Vertical wood slats, matte charcoal backing panel, floating console, indirect LED cove backlighting.
