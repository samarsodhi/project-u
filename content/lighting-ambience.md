# Lighting & Ambience

Layered lighting strategy combining museum-grade CRI 95+ recessed ceiling spots for fine art, 24V dot-free COB mirror halos, solid timber bedside reading lamps, and smart E27 ambient bulbs.

> **Lighting Principles:** Warm white (2700–3000K) throughout living and bedrooms. All smart bulbs pair via Thread/Zigbee. Dimmable drivers pair with trailing-edge dimmers to eliminate coil whine.

## Architectural Coordination & Lux Limits
* **False Ceiling Lighting Standard:** For ambient recessed ceiling downlights, perimeter cove illumination, and trimless mud-in profiles, refer directly to [[false-ceiling-lighting]].
* **Art Illumination Limits:** Maintain illuminance $\le 150\text{ lux}$ for oil canvases and $\le 50\text{ lux}$ for delicate paper/watercolors to prevent thermal or photochemical fading.
* **Electrical Switching:** Electrician must wire art ceiling spots on a dedicated switch leg routed through an `Aqara Dimmer Switch H2` or a ceiling-hatch `Shelly Dimmer Gen3 / Wozart` Matter trailing-edge module.
* **Driver Dimmability:** Bench-test sample LED spotlight driver with the trailing-edge dimmer prior to ceiling installation to verify zero low-end acoustic buzzing.
