# Project U — The Olympus (Unit B4206)

**Location:** The Olympus, Financial District, Hyderabad (Floor 42 of 44, Tower B)  
**Aesthetic:** Japandi core with warm natural hardwoods, subtle brass accents, textured walls, and dense handwoven textiles.

This site hosts the architectural specifications, verified product recommendations, and high-rise engineering protocols for the apartment fit-out.

---

## 1. ⚙️ High-Rise Engineering & Irreversible Services

Pre-ceiling and pre-flooring infrastructure. Failure to sequence these before architectural closures risks catastrophic flooding, electrical board failures, or tearing down finished ceilings:

* **[[high-rise-engineering-gotchas|High-Rise Engineering Gotchas]]** — Pressure Reducing Valves (PRV 3.0–3.5 bar), Type 2 Surge Protection (SPD), DG harmonics, and high-rise wind mitigations.
* **[[cabinetry-woodwork|Cabinetry & Woodwork]]** — IS 710 BWP plywood, calibrated cores, wardrobe hardware schedule, and ceiling plasterboard.
* **[[plans-drawings|Plans & Drawings]]** — Unit B4206 architectural floor plan, Daikin VRV 10 HP HVAC, plumbing shafts, and electrical DB.
* **[[kitchen-laundry|Kitchen & Laundry]]** — Modular cabinetry, light Indian granite, undermount sinks, hybrid domino hob, 32A lines, and stacked laundry.
* **[[finalized-purchases|Finalized Purchases]]** — Committed major appliances, AV envelope (₹4.30L), dedicated 16A radials, and joinery clearances.
* **[[bathrooms-ventilation|Bathrooms & Ventilation]]** — 10L smart geysers, SS304 flanged curtain rods, floor threshold water bars, silent exhaust CFM, and insect mesh.
* **[[lighting-ambience|Lighting & Ambience]]** — Architectural CRI 95+ COB spots, 3000K CCT zoning, remote driver enclosures, and 24V dot-free mirror halos.
* **[[false-ceiling-lighting|False Ceiling Lighting]]** — Recessed COB downlights, perimeter cove strips, trimless slots, and driver service hatches.
* **[[smart-home-automation|Smart Home & Automation]]** — In-wall Matter/Shelly relays, 43mm deep backboxes, neutral line runs, and BLDC fan regulator bypass.
* **[[wifi-internet|Wi-Fi & Internet]]** — Pure copper Cat6 cabling, ceiling PoE access points, OpenWrt gateway, and DC UPS backup.
