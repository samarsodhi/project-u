## 2. MEP & Civil Installation Checklist

### Electrical & Cabling
- **Living TV Console:** Provide a 50mm smooth-bore PVC conduit inside the TV panel down to the base unit. Ensure direct eARC connection to HDMI 3 on the Sony TV.
- **Induction & Built-In Oven:** Pull two independent, unshared 16A radial circuits from the distribution board with individual 16A MCBs.
- **Microwave Point:** Provide a dedicated 16A/6A modular socket in the tall-unit appliance shelf for the solo microwave (~1,100 W input).
- **Utility Room:** Provide dedicated 16A socket for washer (2,300 W peak) and separate 10A/16A socket for heat pump dryer (600 W).
- **Study & Home Office Printer:** Provide a dedicated 6A point near Samar's WFH desk; onboard Brother HL-L2440DW to the Flint 2 wireless network.

### Joinery & Clearances
- **Kitchen Hob Cutout:** Ensure internal cutout corners have a 5–10 mm radius. Avoid sharp 90° right angles in granite.
- **Refrigerator Niche:** Minimum cabinet cavity: 980 mm (W) × 750 mm (D) × 1,980 mm (H). Maintain 50 mm rear air gap and 35 mm side gaps for full 110° door swing clear of 600 mm base cabinetry.
- **Built-in Oven Niche:** Standard tall-unit niche: 575–597 mm (H) × 560 mm (W) × 550 mm (D) with open rear ventilation channel.
- **Microwave Shelf:** Minimum clear shelf dimensions: 600 mm (W) × 450 mm (D) × 400 mm (H) with open front and rear ventilation gap.
- **Laundry Stacking:** Secure dryer over washer using the official Bosch WTZ27400 stacking bracket. Never stack without mechanical coupling.

### Plumbing & Water Safety
- **Washer Inlet:** Minimum 0.3 bar, maximum 10 bar dynamic pressure.
- **Scale Protection:** Install an inline polyphosphate or catalytic scale-prevention cartridge on the cold-water inlet line to prevent mineral crust on heater coils.
- **Drinking Water RO Point:** Connect LG PuriCare WW184ETB to builder's high-level splashback bibcock and 6A power point. Fit an external transparent 10" spun polypropylene sediment pre-filter and point-of-use PRV (Zoloto Art. 1040B forged brass or inline 1/4" PRV set to 2.5–3.0 bar). Route reject water line to drain tailpiece with an air gap $\ge 50\text{ mm}$ above the P-trap weir.
