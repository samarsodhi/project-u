### Floor 42 Evacuation & Life-Safety Protocol

> [!CRITICAL]
> Unit B4206 sits ~126m above ground level. External fire service ladders cannot reach Floor 42. Self-reliance, immediate stairwell access, and physical smoke discipline are non-negotiable.

---

#### 1. Room-by-Room Evacuation & Assembly

- **Primary Route:** Main Door → Internal Pressurised Fire Stairwell → Ground Level.
- **Assembly Point:** Security Main Gate (Ground Level). Never use elevators during a fire or structural alert.

| Room | Immediate Action | Evacuation Path | Assembly Gate Protocol |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Master BR** | Secure Kabir; grab phones | Master door → Living corridor → Main door | Hold Kabir by hand; proceed down fire stair |
| **BR-01 (WFH)** | Drop electronics; leave immediately | BR-01 door → Living corridor → Main door | Unplug desk power only if instantaneous |
| **BR-02** | Check room clear; exit | BR-02 door → Living corridor → Main door | Walk briskly; do not carry loose baggage |
| **BR-03 (Gurudwara)** | Exit door immediately | BR-03 door → Master corridor → Main door | Move Saroop only if immediate zero-threat |
| **Kitchen** | Shut off PNG gas knob | Kitchen door → Dining → Main door | Never throw water on oil; shut knob and exit |
| **Living / Sit-out** | Close sit-out slider to stop draft | Living → Main door | Clear path for children/elders |

---

#### 2. Main Door Grab-Bag Checklist

*Kept in a dedicated go-bag mounted inside the main entryway console.*

- **Smoke & Air Defense:** 4× N95 masks + 4× small cotton face towels (dampen before stair descent).
- **Light & Signaling:** 2× high-lumen tactical LED torches + spare batteries + 2× safety whistles.
- **First Aid & Meds:** Compact trauma kit (burn cream, bandages, antiseptic) + essential family prescription meds.
- **Survival & Docs:** ₹5,000 cash (small notes) + laminated identity/RERA/insurance cards + 20,000 mAh charged powerbank.

---

#### 3. High-Rise DG Switchover & Power Gaps

Floor 42 runs on automatic dual-source power with dedicated common DG backup:

| Power Scenario | Behavior & Gap | Operational Action |
|---|---|---|
| **Grid Outage Bridge (10–30s)** | 10–30s dark window until DG fires. Sub-second network drops unless buffered. | APC 650VA UPS buffers router and smart hub seamlessly. Stand clear of heavy equipment. |
| **DG Active State** | Feeds critical dedicated circuits (1 fridge, 1 lighting loop, network, VRF standby). | Avoid running secondary inductive loads or washing machines on DG bridge. |
| **Generator Failure / Fault** | Dual outage without auto-pickup. | Check DB panel indicators. Contact Facility Management immediately. Never attempt manual tie-in. |
| **Grid Restoration** | DG auto-isolates and utility switches in smoothly. | Verify home switches and VRF reset cleanly. |

---

#### 4. Pre-Move-in Facility Sign-Offs

Verify the following directly with Olympus facility engineering prior to occupancy:
- **Stairwell Pressurisation:** Confirm positive pressure fans operate automatically on alarm to keep stairwells smoke-free.
- **Earth Resistance:** Measure earth-loop impedance at B4206 DB panel ($<1\,\Omega$) for lightning and SPD clamp safety.
- **DG Pickup Time:** Document verified facility transfer speed ($<10\text{s}$). Tape emergency contact card inside main DB cabinet.
