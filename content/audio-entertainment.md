## Architectural & Site Integration Requirements
* **Homz Drawing TV Unit Coordination (East Wall):** Drop the faux-marble `4929 LU` panel and black accent boxes. Finish East TV wall in `Morning Glory (0765)` mineral limewash to kill North window sidelight reflections. Mount monolithic floating credenza (`424 1D Cinnamon Hickory`, 18mm BWP marine ply) on M10 expansion French cleats at 200–220mm FFL. Run a 50mm smooth-bore internal PVC conduit from TV bracket to cabinet interior.
* **Atmos Acoustic Envelope:** Mount the 65" Sony TV with $\ge 150\text{ mm}$ clear vertical gap above the console deck. Isolate the Sonos Arc Ultra ($1,178\text{ mm}$ wide) on the front deck with zero decorative clutter so upward Sound Motion Atmos channels stay 100% unobstructed.
* **Sacred Room Silence (BR-03 Gurudwara):** Strictly zero speakers, microphones, or audio electronics in BR-03. Reverent acoustic silence maintained throughout.
