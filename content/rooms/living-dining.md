# Living & Dining

Covers loose seating, dining table, foyer storage, and architectural media joinery across the main entertainment and family zones.

> **Site & Elevator Constraint:** Olympus service lift car clear depth is ~2.1m. Mandate knock-down (KD) bolted assemblies for large tables and 3-seater sofas to avoid stairwell hoisting.
> **Finishes Reference:** Wall colors reference [[curtains-paint-finishes#definitive-room-by-room-color-palette]]. BLDC ceiling fan rules reference [[smart-home-automation#bldc-ceiling-fan-specifications--siting-rules]].
