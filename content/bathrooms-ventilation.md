# Bathrooms & Ventilation

Master technical specification for the 4 bathrooms: 1× 15L (Master Ensuite) + 3× 10L (Secondary) horizontal storage geysers, non-cramped shower containment (SS304 flanged curtain rods + floor water bars), whisper-quiet **150 mm** ventilation exhaust with sealed backdraft shutters, and high-rise insect fenestration.

> **Design Decision:** Heavy glass cubicle doors are omitted to prevent bathrooms from feeling cramped and eliminate scale-scrubbing. Wall-flanged SS304 rods with weighted drapes and bonded floor threshold bars contain overspray without puncturing waterproofing.

## Engineering Sizing & Operational Best Practices

### 1. Geyser Recovery Time & Flow Benchmarks
* **10L Benchmark (A. O. Smith Elegance Slim Neo):** Heats from 20 °C to 55 °C in ~11 minutes at 2.0 kW. Provides ~3.5–4.5 minutes of continuous hot shower flow at 6 L/min. Ideal for compact false ceiling voids (310 mm diameter) and quick individual showers.
* **15L Benchmark (A. O. Smith HAS-X1 / Elegance Slim Neo 15L / Havells Fabia Slim 15L):** Heats from 20 °C to 55 °C in ~17 minutes at 2.0 kW. Provides ~5.5–6.5 minutes of continuous hot shower flow at 6 L/min. Requires 338–355 mm vertical ceiling clearance.
* **Electrical Load Management:** Stagger wake-up heating schedules by 15 minutes across the 4 bathrooms to keep concurrent electrical load within DG backup capacity.
* **False Ceiling Geyser Leak Protocol (MFV vs Local PRV):** Factory-supplied Multi-Function Valves (MFVs) are mandatory. Reject local brass PRVs above false ceilings (hidden weep hazard). Plumber must clamp a 10 mm heat-resistant vinyl tube to the MFV discharge spout and route it through the ceiling into the shower area; never leave MFV spouts open over plastic ceiling panels.

### 2. Shower Curtain & Wet-Zone Containment Protocol
* **Hardware Mandate:** Screw-mounted AISI 304 stainless steel flanged rods (1.2 mm wall thickness); reject spring-tension rods and chrome-plated mild steel.
* **Floor Barrier:** Bond a solid stone/acrylic water threshold bar using epoxy; do not core drill through bathroom floor waterproofing membranes.
* **Curtain Drop:** Hem curtain 15 mm above shower floor and weight bottom seam so drafts do not blow wet fabric against the user.
